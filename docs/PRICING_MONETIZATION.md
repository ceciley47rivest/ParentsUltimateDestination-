## Pricing Strategy Rationale

### Why $49.99/year Annual?

1. **Extremely Competitive**
   - Parents pay $10-20/month per subscription app
   - We consolidate 5-10 apps into ONE
   - $49.99/year = $4.17/month (vs $50-200/year for competing solutions)

2. **Psychological Pricing**
   - $49.99 feels like a value
   - Annual billing reduces decision fatigue
   - Aligns with "eliminate subscription fatigue" mission

3. **Math Works**
   - Premium family app average: $9.99-14.99/month
   - Annual savings messaging: "Save $70+ this year!"
   - Still profitable with good unit economics

4. **Conversion Friendly**
   - Low barrier to entry
   - Feels like an impulse purchase
   - Clear value proposition vs. free trial

## FAQs for Customers

### Q: Why $49.99/year? That seems almost too cheap!
**A:** We believe parents are drowning in subscriptions. By consolidating 5-10 apps into one, we can offer incredible value at an incredibly low price. It's our mission to eliminate subscription fatigue, not add to it.

### Q: Can I cancel anytime?
**A:** Absolutely! No questions asked. We'd love to know if something could be better, but we never lock you in.

### Q: What if I can't commit to a year?
**A:** Choose monthly billing at $9.99/month. Same features, same quality—just more flexible.

### Q: Is there a free plan forever?
**A:** We offer a 7-day free trial to explore everything. After that, a subscription helps us keep the lights on and continue building amazing features.

### Q: What happens when my trial ends?
**A:** We'll send you reminders on days 3, 5, and 7. On day 8, if you haven't subscribed, you'll lose access. But you can subscribe anytime and reactivate.

### Q: Do you offer family/student discounts?
**A:** Not yet, but it's something we're exploring based on customer feedback!

### Q: Is my payment information secure?
**A:** Yes! We use industry-standard encryption and Stripe's PCI-compliant payment processing. We never store your full card details.
